Summer Fashion Reel Series — 3 reels showcasing trending outfits

The Challenge

Myntra needed to showcase their summer fashion collection to a 18-28 female audience in India. They needed authentic styling content that didn't feel like a traditional ad, with measurable engagement and saves to drive traffic to their platform.

Vanya created 3 reels featuring thrift store finds and Myntra pieces styled together. Each reel used trending audio, Delhi-based locations, and natural storytelling that felt like a friend sharing outfit ideas rather than an ad.
